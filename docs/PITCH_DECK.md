# AdTrust CXAI — Investor Pitch Deck

*Speaker-ready slide content. One `##` = one slide.*

---

## 1 — AdTrust CXAI
**Trust-Aware Affective Customer Intelligence & Autonomous Journey Orchestration.**
An AI-native operating system for customer engagement that understands how customers
*feel*, refuses to act on *fake* signals, and proves *causal* impact.

## 2 — The problem
MarTech optimizes clicks and last-touch attribution. It is blind to the two things
that actually move lifetime value:
- **How the customer feels** — so it over-messages the frustrated and the about-to-churn.
- **Whether the signal is real** — so budget flows to bots, synthetic personas, and click fraud.
Result: wasted spend, eroded trust, "personalization" that's correlation, not cause.

## 3 — Why now
Foundation models make real-time affect inference cheap; agentic orchestration makes
autonomous, explainable decisioning practical; boards now demand *incremental* ROI and
brand-safety/trust guarantees. The pieces exist — nobody has composed them into one
trust-aware, affect-aware decision layer.

## 4 — The product
A continuously-evolving **Emotional Digital Twin** of every customer drives a closed
loop: ingest → feel (10 emotions) → assess trust → forecast intent → decide
(9 agents) → explain → measure causal lift → learn. The trust gate can choose to **do
nothing** to protect the customer and the budget — inaction as a first-class action.

## 5 — Demo (what's live today)
A single service boots in under two seconds with no external dependencies and serves an
operator console over a seeded customer roster:
- Loyal customer → advocacy stage → *recommend*, high trust.
- At-risk customer → frustration → *trigger retention*, churn risk surfaced.
- Bot persona → **flagged** → *do_nothing*, five-step protective trace.
Every decision shows attributions + plain-language rationale + the full agent trace.

## 6 — Technology moat
- **Affect as first-class state** — emotion conditions both action and reward.
- **Trust gates action** — fraud/authenticity can veto engagement.
- **Causal, not correlational** — uplift + counterfactuals via a holdout arm.
- **Explainable by construction** — attributions and agent traces on every call.
- **Swap-seam architecture** — RoBERTa affect, GNN trust, MAPPO/QMIX policy, Neo4j/Qdrant
  drop in behind stable interfaces without touching the decision loop.

## 7 — Research credibility
Framed for AAAI/KDD/WWW: six contributions spanning emotional digital twins, trust-aware
intelligence, affective RL, multi-agent journey optimization, causal marketing
intelligence, and explainable customer decisions. Paper draft and full architecture ship
with the platform.

## 8 — Market
Customer engagement / CDP / MarTech is a multi-tens-of-billions category; trust & safety
and marketing-measurement budgets are adjacent and growing. Wedge: **trust + affect**
where incumbents only have behavior. Land in lifecycle/growth and trust-&-safety teams,
expand to the executive measurement layer.

## 9 — Business model
Usage-based platform fee (per active twin / per decision) + premium modules
(real-model affect, GNN fraud graph, causal measurement) + enterprise tier (SSO,
multi-tenant, channel-execution connectors, dedicated VPC).

## 10 — Roadmap
- **v0.2** real transformer affect, Qdrant look-alike, Postgres persistence.
- **v0.3** MAPPO/QMIX policy, Neo4j fraud graph, Double-ML causal upgrade.
- **v1.0** Next.js console, multi-tenant, channel connectors, CI/CD, SOC2 path.

## 11 — Ask
Raising to harden the production model seams, ship channel-execution connectors, and land
three design-partner enterprises in lifecycle marketing and trust & safety. Capital goes
to applied ML, data engineering, and design-partner success.

## 12 — Closing
Behavior tells you *what* a customer did. AdTrust CXAI tells you *how they feel*, *whether
to believe it*, and *what to do next* — autonomously, causally, and explainably.
